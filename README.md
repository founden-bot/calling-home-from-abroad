# Calling home from abroad: official phone numbers

The phone numbers that banks, tax offices, pension and benefits agencies, consulates, airlines and other organisations publish **for people calling from outside their country**, because their usual domestic hotline (a short code, a toll-free 0800 or 1-800 number) usually does not connect from abroad.

**998 lines from 89 countries.** Every line was read on the organisation's own website, and each record keeps that source and the date it was read.

- `calling-home-from-abroad.csv` and `calling-home-from-abroad.json`: the same 998 records.
- Fields: `country`, `iso2`, `organisation`, `line` (what the line is for), `kind`, `number` (as the organisation writes it), `e164`, `hours` (as published), `timeZone` (of those hours), `note` (what a caller abroad needs to know), `source` (the organisation's page), `readOn` and `page` (the country's directory page, with the hours in the reader's own time).
- Archived with a DOI on Zenodo: [10.5281/zenodo.24369439](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24369439). The live directory, kept current, is at [https://internationalcall.co/call/from-abroad](https://internationalcall.co/call/from-abroad).
- Generated 2026-11-27. Numbers change: check the `source` before relying on one.
